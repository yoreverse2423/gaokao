## 短文

**The Lost Lunch Box**

On Monday morning, Li Wei walked into the classroom with a worried face. He had lost his lunch box on the way to school. He did not want to tell anyone, so he kept quiet and sat down.

At noon, his classmate Chen Jie saw him sitting alone. "Where is your lunch?" she asked. Li Wei hesitated for a moment. He did not like to ask for help, but his stomach was making noise. At last he told her the truth. Chen Jie did not laugh. She opened her own box and gave him half of her rice and an egg.

"It's not much," she said, "but we can manage together."

Li Wei felt relief wash over him. He had thought the whole day would be bad. Instead, it turned into a warm one. He said he was very grateful.

After school, the two of them looked near the bus stop. Under a bench, Li Wei found his lunch box.

That evening, Li Wei told his mother what had happened. "I was worried about being hungry," he said, "but a friend helped me first." His mother smiled and said, "A small kindness can change a whole day."

## 题目

**1. What did Li Wei do at first when Chen Jie asked him about his lunch?**
A. He told her the truth at once.
B. He hesitated and did not tell her the truth at once.
C. He asked her to buy him some food.
D. He left the classroom without a word.

**2. What did Chen Jie give Li Wei at noon?**
A. A whole lunch box of her own.
B. Some money to buy lunch.
C. Half of her rice and an egg.
D. A note from her mother.

**3. Where did Li Wei find his lunch box after school?**
A. In the classroom.
B. Near the school gate.
C. Under a bench near the bus stop.
D. At his home.

**4. The word "relief" in the sentence "Li Wei felt relief wash over him" is closest in meaning to ______.**
A. a feeling of being tired and sleepy
B. a feeling that a worry has gone away
C. a feeling of being angry with someone
D. a feeling of being hungry again
