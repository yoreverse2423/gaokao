# 第4周语篇小练（原创基础训练·非真题）

本练习是基础小样本，只有2个空和4个选项，不代表完整高考七选五（不是5空7选项）。学校已有同类七选五作业则只做学校那份，不额外加量。本块为可选。
建议在原有周六块内完成：阅读约8–10分钟、作答约10分钟、回看约5–8分钟，合计不超过30分钟；做不完就停。用时未经实测。先标空前空后的线索再选，不先按大意乱配。不查词也可以先做，不另抄词表。

## 短文

Our class keeps a small wooden box near the classroom door. We put unused pens, pencils and erasers into it so that anyone can take one when they forget their own. Nobody is asked to buy new ones just for the box. (1) ______. After morning classes, the monitor checks the box and makes sure it is not too messy.

At first, a few students said nobody would really use it. (2) ______. For example, last Thursday, three classmates forgot their pens before a quiz, and they all found one in the box. The idea is simple, but it has already helped us more than once. Now some students even remind others to return unused pens at the end of the day, so the box can help the next person.

## 题目

**1. 从下列选项中为 (1)、(2) 两处各选一句，每个选项最多使用一次。**
A. These items come from students who have extras at home.
B. However, the box soon proved useful.
C. They are kept in a locked drawer in the teachers' office.
D. Therefore, we stopped putting stationery into the box.
