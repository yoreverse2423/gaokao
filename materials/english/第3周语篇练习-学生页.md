# 第3周语篇小练（原创基础训练·非真题）

本练习降低了语法难度，不代表完整高考语法填空难度。共4个动词空和1道主旨题；第3空已给助动词，是分步练习。学校已有同类作业则只做学校那份，不额外加量。
建议在原有周末块内完成：动词空约15分钟、主旨题约10分钟、回看约5分钟；最多30分钟，做不完就停。用时未经实测。不查词也可以先做，不另抄词表。

## 短文

Last spring, our class started a small garden behind the school library. At first, the ground was hard, and we needed help. Our science teacher showed us how to dig the soil. Every group (1) ______ (get) a small piece of land. We worked in small teams and shared the tools with each other.

The garden now (2) ______ (have) six rows of vegetables and flowers. Every morning, some students water the plants before class. Some old boxes are (3) ______ (use) as plant pots. We keep a simple notebook beside the door to record changes in the plants.

Last week, a group of younger students visited our garden. We (4) ______ (tell) them about our work and gave them some seeds. They asked many questions and wanted to grow plants at home. Our little garden is not big, but it brings many people together.

## 题目

**5. What is the main idea of the passage?**
A. A class garden connects students through growing and sharing.
B. A science teacher spends every lesson teaching students to dig.
C. Younger students start a new garden behind their own library.
D. Old boxes are better for growing plants than all other pots.
