## School Bike Repair Day — Notice

Dear students,

The school will hold a Bike Repair Day on **Saturday, March 20**, from **9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.** The activity will take place in the **parking lot behind the science building**, not in the main hall as last year.

Any student in Grades 7–9 can bring one bike for a free check. Grade 6 students may come to watch but cannot bring a bike this time. You do not need to sign up before the day, but you must bring your **student card** and arrive before **10:30 a.m.** so that each bike can be checked in time.

Volunteers from the school bike club will help with simple repairs, such as fixing brakes and putting air in tyres. They cannot change gears or repair electric bikes. If your bike needs new parts, please buy them before the day and bring them with you. The school will not provide parts.

**Important change**: Because of a teachers' meeting, the starting time is now **9:30 a.m.**, not 9:00 a.m. The activity will still end at 12:00 p.m. Please tell your parents about the new starting time.

If it rains heavily, the activity will move to the **covered walkway near the school gate**. A message will be put on the school noticeboard by 7:30 a.m. on March 20.

School Bike Club
